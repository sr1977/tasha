# Research: Candidate Exercises for the Tasha Pool

**Date:** 2026-08-02 · **Depth:** standard · **Constraint:** household kit only
(bodyweight / dumbbells / one medicine ball); categories upper/lower/core;
no duplicates of the current 23-move pool.

## Gap analysis

Current pool: 8 upper (4 bw, 4 db), 8 lower (4 bw, 4 db), 7 core (5 bw, 2 mb).
Biggest gaps: **medicine ball upper and lower** (zero moves), core has no
anti-rotation/isometric variety beyond plank, and lower body has no
explosive move.

## Candidates

### Upper — bodyweight
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Plank-ups | Plank to push-up and back; shoulders + triceps, no kit |
| Diamond push-ups | Close-grip push-up; tricep-dominant variation |
| Wide push-ups | Chest-dominant variation; easy roster spread |

### Upper — dumbbells
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Lateral raises | Isolates the side delts; nothing in pool hits them |
| Overhead tricep extensions | Only tricep iso move with load |
| Hammer curls | Forearm/brachialis complement to bicep curls |
| Arnold press | Rotating press; fuller shoulder coverage than strict press |
| Renegade rows | Row from plank; upper pull + heavy core demand |
| Floor flyes | Chest isolation lying down; safe without a bench |

### Upper — medicine ball
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Med ball push-ups | One or both hands on the ball; instability builds pressing control |
| Overhead ball slams | The classic power move — needs a non-bouncy ball and a forgiving floor |

### Lower — bodyweight
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Squat jumps | Pool's first explosive lower move |
| Side lunges | Lateral plane — everything current is front-to-back |
| Single-leg glute bridges | Unilateral progression of glute bridges |
| Calf raises | Nothing hits calves; easy low-skill station |
| Bulgarian split squats | Rear foot on a chair/bench; brutal unilateral quad/glute |

### Lower — dumbbells
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Thrusters | Squat into overhead press; conditioning staple |
| Sumo squats | Wide stance, inner thigh/glute bias |
| Dumbbell swings | Hip-hinge power, kettlebell-swing pattern with a dumbbell |

### Lower — medicine ball
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Squat to press (ball) | Squat holding the ball, press overhead on the way up; no wall needed |
| Wall balls | Squat + throw at a wall — only if the training space has a sturdy wall |

### Core — bodyweight
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Mountain climbers | Dynamic core + conditioning; circuit staple |
| Side plank | First anti-lateral-flexion move in the pool |
| Dead bugs | Anti-extension control; pairs well with the nastier stations |
| Hollow hold | Isometric alternative to plank; harder ceiling |
| V-ups | Dynamic progression of leg raises + sit-ups |

### Core — medicine ball
| Move | Why |
|---|---|
| Ball V-ups | V-up passing the ball hands-to-feet |
| Weighted ball sit-ups | Sit-ups holding the ball — loads the existing pattern |

## Notes
- Flagged as space-dependent: overhead slams (floor), wall balls (wall).
- Upright rows were considered and dropped — common shoulder-impingement complaints.
- Reverse/walking lunges dropped as too close to the existing Lunges.
